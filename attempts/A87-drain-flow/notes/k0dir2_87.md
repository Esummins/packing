## Conventions and caveats

* Acceptance is judged by the strict test **at the floor** on the strictified (`fjpolish87.inflate`) iterate, so every point counted K0-BLOCKED is a genuine packing.
* The confined SQP pins the two deepest intruders per corner to depth >= 1.02*delta so that the strictify step cannot push an accepted iterate back below the floor.  Reported sides are therefore UPPER bounds on the true infimum at each floor.
* The exact test uses the sound INNER slot of k0dir87 (a rational rectangle contained in the true unit slot for every realisation of the interval bounding box), with the bar at the floor: MEETS iff `gap.b <= -delta`, does NOT meet iff `gap.a > -delta`.
* This is a numerical SEARCH, not a proof of non-existence.


## CORRECTION: the floor-wise bound is monotone, the search is not

K0-BLOCKED(delta) is NESTED DECREASING in delta, so a packing certified strictly
K0-BLOCKED at a coarse floor is certified at every finer floor. The record found by
the 1e-3 search (`d1em03_s0050`, certified side 3.990748514985) has min certified
second-meeter depth 1.020e-3, hence it certifies at 1e-6 and 1e-4 as well. The 1e-6
and 1e-4 searches simply failed to find that basin; their 4.0000000 / 4.0000044
endpoints are SEARCH outcomes, not properties of the region.

### Is the 3.9907 record an eta-disturbance of a K0-FREE packing?

Partly. Certified second-meeter depths (BL BR TL TR) =
7.673e-02 / 1.020e-03 / 9.876e-03 / 1.020e-03 against a 1e-3 floor. BR and TR sit
exactly on the constraint pin (1.02*delta) and TL is within 10x of it, so THREE of the
four corners are held by marginal second meeters: an eta ~ 1e-3 perturbation at BR or TR
frees that corner and the packing becomes K0-FREE. Only BL (7.7e-2, 77x the floor) is a
genuine deep 2-crowd. So the sub-4 witness is an eta-thin, not a robust, K0-BLOCKED point
-- which is precisely why the 1e-2 floor cannot reach below 4.

### The 3.9907 vs 3.9990 discrepancy for `d1em03_s0050`

Two different stages of the same seed. `sigma_best_blocked` = 3.999041804 in
`results/k0dir2_87.jsonl` is the endpoint of the per-seed DESCENT phase (float side of the
confined SQP, 25 steps). The DEEP re-run then walks the pinned depth down the ladder
4e-3 -> 2e-3 -> 1.3e-3 -> 1.02e-3 and reaches 3.990748514985. The CERTIFIED number is the
latter: 3.990748514985 is `verify_exact`'s interval upper bound on the enclosing side of
the INFLATED rational configuration, and it is the only one backed by a certificate.

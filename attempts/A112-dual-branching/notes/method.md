# A112 — method (how the region tree is built and what was actually measured)

## Discipline

* **Two processes at most**, BLAS-pinned (`code/blas1.py` is imported before numpy in every entry
  point, through `a112lib`), one shard each (`code/run_all.sh 0 2`, `code/run_all.sh 1 2`).
  Every node LP and every certificate run is preceded by `a112lib.gate` — free commit > 6144 MB
  **and** free physical > 3072 MB, twice in a row, 15 s poll. A96's and A61's campaigns were
  running throughout and were never touched.
* **READ-ONLY on every campaign.** A57/A60/A61/A96/A107/A111/A113/A114 node jsons are *copied*
  into `results/nodes/<region>.src.json` (`a112lib.copy_node`) before anything opens them for a
  solve; their results files are only read. **A57 is paused and none of its drivers was launched.**
* **Frozen verifiers byte-identical.** `verify_case.py` sha256 `521cb01f…`, `certify.py`
  `05df5c69…`, `caselp.py` `4e313d7f…`, `lpbox.py` `36cddcef…`, `trim57.py` `649fc37d…`,
  `pinguard.py` `5e45aec0…` — each a byte-for-byte copy of the file A57 runs today, verified by
  `sha256sum` against `attempts/A57-shadow-trees/code/`. (`pinguard.py` is A57's *current* file,
  i.e. the one carrying A96's `17aa7a87` range assertions, not A111's older copy.)
  `verify_param.py` sha256 `0c58485c…`, the copy A96 froze.
* **The dense-sampling rule** (A111's standing rule): no LP-free geometric count is reported before
  ≥ 10⁴ sampled pose pairs confirm it. Every free-kill fire is sampled at branch time
  (`a112lib.sample_pair`, 400 pairs, per-node seed) and again at the end with an independent seed
  and a 5× larger sample (`code/canary112.py`).
* **Tilt intervals normalised to [0, 90] and the core half-side asserted ≤ 1/2** before any core is
  used (`a112lib.assert_core_half_side` → `octagon.safe_kill_core`), which is A111 bug B5's fix
  carried forward; `trim57.dead_by_pin` and `pinguard.tight_a` both refuse an unnormalised
  interval outright, so a reflected bin can never reach the envelopes they are stated on.

## The region tree (`code/regiontree.py`)

**Root** = a stalled node's hypothesis exactly as the campaign emitted it: its class pins (A57's
slack pin cores, half-width δ/√2 with δ = 10⁻²) and its conditioned bin(s), at the campaign's own
side s₀ = 969271/250000 and its own budget. The root's LP result is the campaign's own — no LP is
re-run at the root.

**Branch.** Pick a placement *p* out of the node's OWN LP dual support (`a112lib.support`: the
fractional placements with y > 0, clustered at 0.02 / 2° so a placement re-generated by column
generation counts once) with a pluggable ORDER function, and put a box

    F = [c_p ± r] × [θ_p ± ρ],   (r, ρ) = (0.1, 10°) at depth 0, halved at each level
                                 (A111's radius table; floor 0.0125 / 1.25°).

**Children.**

| child | hypothesis | budget |
|---|---|---|
| YES | `hyp.boxes + [F]` — some square's pose lies in F | budget − 1 |
| NO | `hyp.nofams + [NoFam(F)]` — no square's pose lies in F | budget |

"some square in F or no square in F" is a tautology, so the two children **cover the node exactly**
— that is the whole containment argument, and it is written out per node (see below).

**Soundness of the YES child** (`regiontree.yes_sound`). Dropping the budget by one asserts the box
is witnessed by a square *distinct* from every square already accounted for. Two obligations are
checked before a candidate is ever chosen: `pinguard.classify` must not return `parent` (a pin
placement inside F would let a pin witness it), and F must be **pose-disjoint** from every bin
already in the hypothesis (`octagon.boxes_pose_disjoint`; two boxes sharing a pose may be witnessed
by the same square). A candidate failing either is skipped, never branched on.

**At every YES node, before any LP:**

1. **the octagon box-vs-pin trim** — A57's T5″ (`trim57.trim_box`, byte-identical), applied to every
   conditioned bin on every normalised tilt branch. A bin whose surviving tilt set is empty kills
   the node with no LP and no certificate.
2. **the free pair kill** — A111's CORE-MEET (A96's tight common inscribed core through
   `pinguard.kill_core`, wrapped by `octagon.safe_kill_core`) and Lemma TC (tilt coupling), run
   between **every pair** of conditioned bins and between every bin and every pin. Pin-vs-pin is
   skipped: the class gate already keeps the pins disjoint. Every fire is recorded with the two
   boxes, the rule, and its dense-sampling check.

**Then the CHAIN kill (2b)** — A111's GRAIN-LP applied to the node's own boxes at its own side
(`regiontree.chain_kill`): `t1_yes.selection_census` encloses the eight signed separating-axis
selections of every pair by outward-rounded interval arithmetic; a pair with one possible selection
gives a valid row, a pair with several is dropped (a relaxation, so sound), and `t1_yes.cell_lp`
minimises s with the containment rows at the cell **minimum** half-width. LP infeasible at the
node's own side ⇒ the YES cell is empty. It is a **float LP: a measured kill, not a certified one**
(the certified form is A111's leaf O1–O4), and it is recorded and counted apart from every
certificate. Controls, run before it was wired in: on A111's own S02 boxes with k5_11's pins it
fires at r = 0.005 and 0.002 and not at r = 0.0097, 0.0125, 0.025, 0.05, 0.1 — reproducing A111's
`r < s* − s0 = 0.0097` threshold from the other side; on Trump's own five tilted squares with
trump5's pins it does **not** fire at any radius (lb = s0 exactly, feasible), and
`substrat.trump_realizes` confirms Trump satisfies those hypotheses.

**Then the LP and the CERTIFIER**, under the PI's timeout protocol (2026-09-06):

| stage | what runs | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A57's `solve_node`, unmodified, warm from the parent (rows capped at 20 000), **240 s** | η ≤ −10⁻³ → straight to the certifier, converged or not |
| cert | A57's `certify.py --jobs 1` → the frozen `verify_case.py`, 240 s | PASS → **DEAD-CERT**; FAIL → the node stays **ALIVE** and is branched on |
| 2 | only a node not clearly below budget, escalated to the **900 s cap** | still `|η| < 10⁻⁶` → ALIVE (knife edge) |

**τ is never a verdict.** A kill is a certified PASS, a free (logic) kill, or a chain-LP kill; there
is no "LP-dead" tier. Every node records `flat900_s`, the wall the same node would have cost under a
flat 900 s cap (the same time if the LP converged inside stage 1, otherwise the full cap — A111's
datum: an unconverged child at the 900 s cap ran 938 s and was *still* unconverged), so the two
regimes are compared on identical nodes.

**Resumability.** Every region-tree node has its own record file
`results/rt/<region>/<ordering>/<key>.json` (key = `R` then `Y`/`N` per level), written atomically.
A node whose record exists is never recomputed; `run112.py` additionally skips a region already
present in its results jsonl. The run can be killed at any point and restarted.

## The five ORDER functions (`regiontree.score_candidates`) — all LP-free

For every support placement, four scores are computed with no LP solve anywhere:

* `rows_struck` — the LP **row mass** the YES core K(F) strikes out: Σ y over support placements
  whose square is forced to overlap K(F) (exactly `caselp.Core.depth_many`'s four-axis test, so it
  is the LP's own exemption predicate);
* `nofam_mass` — the **support mass** the NO-family of F removes (`caselp.NoFam.contains`);
* `loadpath_mult` / `loadpath_rank` — the **Fritz-John multiplier** of the shadow's load path: the
  support mass sitting inside a load-path box of the node's own matched shadow, mapped into the
  node's frame by the inverse of A107's `struct_op`; members are ranked by their count of LOADED
  contacts at the shadow's own configuration (`t1_yes.loaded_pairs`, read off the closed-form SAT
  gap), with the load path proper ahead of A111's anchors;
* `pin_wastage` — **PIN WASTAGE** (PI, 2026-09-06): the **area of the pockets** the candidate box
  creates. A container point is *usable* if some tilt admits a unit square centred there that fits
  between the walls and overlaps none of the blockers — the class pins' cores, the other
  conditioned bins' cores, and K(F). `wastage(F) = area(unusable with F) − area(unusable without
  F)`, on a 0.02 centre grid × 19 tilts (`a112lib.Wastage`). Pure geometry; it is a branching
  score, never a certified quantity, and no kill is ever claimed from it.
* `unusable_area` (the variant the brief asks to report): the mean over F's tilt interval of the
  exact area of the octagon K(F) ⊕ (−Q(0,θ)) — the centre region F's core forbids to every other
  square, by convex-polygon intersection.

The orderings are then:

| ordering | key |
|---|---|
| `loadpath` | in a load-path box first; load path before anchors; then the member's loaded-contact count, then its multiplier, then distance |
| `stranded` | `rows_struck + nofam_mass` descending |
| `hybrid` | the load-path member (distance ≤ 0.25) that strands the most |
| `wastage` | `pin_wastage` descending, `stranded` as tie-break |

`wastage_vs_rowmass_spearman` and `wastage_vs_stranded_spearman` are computed at every branching
node over its full candidate list, so the PI's question — how the wastage score relates to the row-
mass score — is answered per node, not just in aggregate.

## Two structural tendencies the measurement confirmed (neither is a theorem)

**A YES child of a support-guided branch usually sits at τ = budget exactly.** The branch box is on
the parent's own LP support, so conditioning one more square there tends to cost exactly one unit of
budget and buy exactly one unit of τ. Measured over the S02 region trees: **116 of 126 YES-child LPs
(92 %) land at η = 0 to the last digit**, and the other 10 go below budget and certify. So the YES
side is *mostly* a job for the free kills (trim, pair kill, chain LP), but not always — and both of
A112's genuine kills are (YES certified) ∧ (NO certified). The NO side is the productive one: 44 of
104 NO-child LPs (42 %) go below budget, down to η = −1.0.

**On an integral support the free pair kill cannot fire between two branch boxes** — the placements
are then pairwise disjoint. It fires between a branch box and the node's own conditioned bin, or
against a pin (A111's finding). Measured: of A112's 10 fires, 4 are bin-vs-pin at a root and **4 are
bin-vs-bin between boxes the region tree itself introduced, at depths 2–4** — which is the second
conditioned square A112 exists to produce, and it appears only once the tree has branched twice.

## The containment record (`regiontree.containment`)

Every region tree writes its kill claim as "old box X dead by region tree X/O" with three
obligations checked explicitly and stored in `results/t2_*.jsonl` under `containment`:

* **C1** — the root hypothesis IS the old node's hypothesis: the boxes, the NO-families, the budget
  and the side are compared against the campaign's own json (`json.dumps(..., sort_keys=True)`
  equality). The region tree therefore covers the old box exactly, not a relaxation of it.
* **C2** — every internal node's two children carry the **same** branch box F, one as a core box
  with the budget dropped by one and one as the NO-family of the same rectangle (clipped to
  [0.5, s−0.5], which only *shrinks* the NO side, i.e. only weakens the NO child). A tautologous
  split, so the union of the leaves is the root.
* **C3** — every leaf is dead (DEAD-LOGIC, DEAD-CERT or DEAD-CLASS).

The kill transfers iff C1 ∧ C2 ∧ C3. `transfers` is recorded per region and per ordering.

## The k0 face

A k0 cell (A38's level-1 nodes, A113's un-killable list) has **no pins**, one conditioned bin and
budget 10, so the box-vs-pin trim is vacuous and the only free rule available is the pair kill
between the bin and the new box — which is exactly A113's finding that *the k0 tree has zero logic
kills because it never branches on corners*. The region tree branches on corners by construction.

The brief's k0 YES-side shortcut — "a YES box in a slot cell leaves the k = 0 stratum, and every
k ≥ 1 class is dead at s₀" — is **recorded and counted but NOT taken**, because it does not hold in
the form a box containment can discharge. A53 stratifies by **grid-locked** ("simple") squares:
doubly flush against the walls and the already-locked squares, wall-anchored. A square whose centre
merely lies in a corner cell, even axis-aligned, need not be grid-locked, so "some square's pose lies
in F ⊆ cell C" does **not** imply k ≥ 1. Every YES node whose box lands in a slot cell therefore
carries `class_clause` with `taken: false`, the cell name and whether the tilt interval is inside
A107's 3° "simple" tolerance, and the count is reported — so the value of proving the *sound* form
(K0-FREE/teleport applied to the LP support, which is what `residue_ledger` §4 actually licenses) can
be read straight off. Switching it on is a deliberate flag (`Runner(k0_class_clause=True)`), never a
default.

## Canaries (`code/canary112.py`)

* **Trump's grain is never killed** — `substrat.trump_realizes` (A57's own canary, Trump embedded at
  s₀ as in A53's `check_trump_feasible`) is evaluated on **every** hypothesis A112 declares dead.
  Structurally, no S07 branch is taken at level 1: the region set is S02-explained A57 nodes at
  levels 2–3 and k0 cells, and A111's finding that the S07 branch is unsound at L1 is respected by
  never entering it.
* **A111's two end-to-end kills reproduced** — `k5_11_interior_Y0054_D00` (S02, L2, 5 NO children)
  and `k5_16_interior_Y0984` (S07, L1, 6 NO children): A111's own NO-child node jsons are copied
  (read-only) and re-certified through A112's byte-identical frozen `verify_case.py`.
* **Every free-kill count re-sampled** before it is reported, with an independent seed and 2000
  pairs per fire.

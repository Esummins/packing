within `2.3 h²` of `w`, `h` the cell half-width — second order, like A119's relaxation in the
angle.

**Quantisation.**  Rows sit on the denominator `10¹²`, with the constant lowered by the total
rounding slack over the leaf's box, so an emitted row is implied by the true relaxed row.  The
Farkas bound is then re-evaluated from the emitted integers; the worst margin over all leaves of a
certificate is in `meta.worst_quantised_margin` and the tightest of the ten is
**6.67·10⁻⁷** (`S02 (0.2, 10°)`), five decades above the rounding.  (A126 at `10⁻⁹` had
9.09·10⁻⁸ on the same neighbourhood.)

**The cells.**  Root `t`-cells are multiples of `2⁻²⁴`, rounded INWARD from A119's angle cells and
clipped to `[0, 1]`; bisection halves them, so every bound in every certificate is dyadic.  Boxes
whose angle cell left `[0°, 90°]` are marked `clipped` in `meta.bodies`: `S02`'s `BR(0,1)` in all
four `S02` certificates, `S33`'s `f0` in all three `S33` ones, none in `S07`.

## T1 — the control (`code/control133.py`, `results/control.json`)

Section (a) re-checks the certificate as emitted; section (b) mutates it one leaf (or one envelope
entry) at a time and requires the checker to REJECT; section (c) draws 10⁴ configurations from `N`
at a side in `[s_lo, s0]` and counts the ones that are genuinely packings of `L ∪ P`.

Two mutations are informational rather than required, and for the same reason in both cases — the
mutant is a DIFFERENT but still SOUND certificate, so accepting it is not a hole:

* `drop-row` on a leaf whose Farkas bound clears `s0` with room to spare;
* `halve-y` on an infeasibility RAY (halving a positive ray bound keeps it positive) — those
  leaves are skipped, which is why the `halve-y` denominators are smaller than the sample size.

`env-h` / `env-w` are applied only to entries whose plane is within the bump `10⁻³` of the
function it bounds, for the same reason: measured on `S02 (0.05, 20°)`, 13 of its 312 `w`
envelopes have `≥ 10⁻³` of slack (up to `3.5·10⁻²`, on the wide cell products), and raising those
by `10⁻³` leaves them valid lower bounds.
